      About the painter

      About the school

      Is it a painting or a stage? Gabriel, his wings striped like a rainbow, brings Mary the news that God has chosen her to bear his son. His greeting floats in gold letters by his lips. Mary lifts her hands in surprise, yet agrees, and her answer is painted upside down so heaven can read it. Around them, every arch, window and column of the church is painted with astonishing care.
